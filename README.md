# cookandgasstove

Premium, modern, and responsive website for **Gas Stove Repair & Kitchen Chimney Repair Service Centre Bhopal**.

## Features
- **Modern Luxury Pastel Aesthetic**: Nordic Mint & Soft Champagne Gold theme
- **Exact Layout Replica**: Follows the structure and flow of reference site
- **Key Sections**:
  - Announcement bar with live pulse indicator
  - Navigation bar with brand logo & mobile menu toggle
  - Hero section with trust proof indicators and dual CTAs
  - What We Do (6 service cards with realistic appliance imagery)
  - Why Choose Us (certified technicians, 90-day warranty, transparent pricing)
  - How It Works (step-by-step process)
  - About Us with multi-brand support badges
  - Customer Reviews with interactive slider
  - Contact Us with quick-enquiry callback form connected to WhatsApp
  - Footer with quick directory & back to top
  - Floating WhatsApp & Call Now buttons
- **Responsive**: Desktop, tablet, and mobile optimized
